
Employees receive 20 annual leave days per calendar year.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least 3 working days before the requested leave date.

Employees receive up to 10 medical leave days per year.

Medical leave longer than 3 consecutive days may require supporting documentation.

Employees who leave the company are paid for approved unused annual leave according to company policy.

The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

Normal working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
